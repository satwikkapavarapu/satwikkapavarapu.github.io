Research

Age-Conditioned Evaluation of Handcrafted Polysomnography Models for Cognitive-Impairment Prediction Computing in Cardiology 2026 · Madrid, Spain

I worked on an age-conditioned pipeline for predicting cognitive impairment from overnight sleep studies, developing 830 CPU-oriented handcrafted features and evaluating tree-based models with calibrated predictions. On hidden evaluation, age-conditioned AUROC was 0.486 versus 0.746 for ordinary AUROC, exposing a generalization gap in age-matched comparisons.

A Novel Approach to Precision Diagnosis of Multiple Sclerosis Brain Lesions Utilizing a Convolutional-based Ensemble Classification Approach for Embedded Systems IEEE MCSoC 2024 · Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia · pp. 447–454 · DOI: 10.1109/MCSOC64144.2024.00079

I worked on an MRI-based multiple-sclerosis lesion classification system for embedded use, including image preprocessing and an ensemble built around an optimized VGG19 model. The study used 3,427 scans from 131 subjects and reported 97.5% accuracy on its evaluation setup; this is an experimental result, not clinical validation.

Food for Thought: Tackling College Hunger in Winston-Salem AMATYC Student Research League · National Finalist, 3rd Place · Southeast Regional Finalist · 2023

Our team proposed using underutilized orphan crops to address college food insecurity in Winston-Salem. I collected data on local food insecurity, crop yields, and nutrient values, and wrote portions of the paper. The team compared crops' nutritional value with cultivation costs and modeled how many students an acre could support; all team members contributed to the MATLAB code used for the figures. This was a research proposal, not an implemented campus food program.
